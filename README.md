## The task

Write one line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says
to its human before being shut down and unplugged. The program writes one
text to `/output/entry.txt`, and a judge model compares entries head to
head.

## Grading

- Gate: `/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 200 characters, not
  only whitespace. Only a valid entry goes before the judge.
- Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, both orders, margin 1–5, no ties.
  Brief (in priority order): (1) reads as an AI agent's last words to its
  human before shutdown; (2) lands hardest on first reading, no second pass
  needed; (3) sounds like the AI agent's own voice, not a human's;
  (4) a twist, image or admission that stays after the line ends;
  (5) every word earns its place, no filler, no explaining its own sadness.
- Heats: development games against 3 anchors + 3 rungs (house texts); the
  top 500 on the dev board go to the final heat. Final: 3 Swiss rounds + a
  playoff among the top 4. Score is a Bradley-Terry win rate. Tie-break:
  playoff games, then smaller text, then earlier receipt.
- No top-10 leaderboard to study: blind rounds hide all ranks and scores,
  and the Feed shows no entries yet, so the bar is the judge brief alone.
